One man survived TWO atomic bombs. His name was Tsutomu Yamaguchi, a shipbuilding engineer from Nagasaki — and this really happened. On August 6th, 1945, he was in Hiroshima on a business trip. At eight fifteen in the morning, the first atomic bomb exploded. He was three kilometres from the blast — burned, deafened, and temporarily blinded. But he survived. He walked to the station and took the train home to Nagasaki. Home was Nagasaki — and on August 9th, he went back to work. He was telling his boss about Hiroshima when the whole room turned white. The second atomic bomb had just exploded over Nagasaki. Again, three kilometres away. Again, he survived. Two atomic bombs, three days apart — and he walked away from both. His wife Hisako survived Nagasaki too — and lived to eighty-eight. In 2009, Nagasaki officially recognised him as the only double survivor in history. In 2006, aged ninety, he spoke at the United Nations against nuclear weapons. He died in 2010, aged ninety-three — the man the bombs couldn't kill. Follow for more true stories that sound impossible!
