He ran a million-pound drug empire — from a quiet street in Sheffield.
His name is Daniel Karim. Thirty-four years old, from Wincobank Close.
For almost four years, police watched his every move.
Detectives in Rotherham built the case, street by street, dealer by dealer.
From 2019, he ran a network of street dealers across Rotherham and Sheffield.
His gang prepared and sold around one million pounds of Class A drugs.
Then, in 2022, officers arrested him.
He pleaded guilty to two counts of conspiracy to supply Class A drugs.
On the 1st of October 2025, Derby Crown Court jailed him for 19 years and 8 months.
Detective Superintendent Nicola Shimwell said officers dismantled the group from the top down.
She said Karim and his associates profited from funnelling harmful drugs onto the streets.
His associates fell too. Omaan Jahanghir, 29, got eleven years.
Nigel Hopewell, 46, got almost seven years. Lee Rocket, 40, got three years and five months.
Four others went to prison with him — more than 35 years behind bars between them all.
Her warning was clear: anyone dealing drugs will be targeted.
